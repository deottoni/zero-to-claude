1
Extract from ChatGPT
6 prompts · copy each one into ChatGPT · paste the answer back into Cowork
How this phase works

You'll do each prompt one at a time — Claude will give you the first one, you paste it into ChatGPT, work it out there until you're happy with the answer, then come back and paste the result into Cowork. Claude will start building your files while you work on the next one. Repeat 6 times.

💡 You can use the prompts below as a reference while you're working in ChatGPT. If ChatGPT gives a vague answer, ask it to be more specific — "give me more detail on #3" works great.
Prompt 1 — Who I Am
▾

This prompt asks ChatGPT to describe you — your name, role, background, industry, and personality. It's the foundation everything else builds on.

Copy → paste into ChatGPT
I'm setting up a new AI assistant and need you to help me export what you know about me. Please answer the following about me based on everything you've learned from our conversations. Be specific — use real details, not generalizations. 1. What is my full name, and what do I do professionally? (job title, role, type of work) 2. Where am I based? (city, country, or general region) 3. How would you describe my professional background in 3-5 sentences? 4. What industry or field do I work in? 5. Am I a business owner, employee, freelancer, or something else? 6. Do I have a business or brand name? If so, what is it? 7. What would you say makes me different or unique in what I do? 8. How would you describe me as a person in 2-3 sentences? (personality, energy, how I come across) Format your answer as a clean list with each question numbered. Be specific and factual — only include what you actually know about me from our conversations, not guesses.
↩ Once ChatGPT answers, read it carefully. If something feels wrong or incomplete, ask ChatGPT to fix it before pasting back into Cowork.
Prompt 2 — My Goals & Desires
▾

This one captures what you're working toward — professional goals, personal aspirations, what success means to you, and what you want more of.

Copy → paste into ChatGPT
I'm setting up a new AI assistant and need you to export what you know about my goals and desires. Based on everything we've discussed, please answer the following: 1. What are my main professional goals right now? (where I want to take my work or business) 2. What are my personal goals or life goals, if I've mentioned any? 3. What does success look like for me — how have I described it? 4. What am I most excited or passionate about in my work? 5. What problems am I trying to solve, either in my business or my life? 6. What have I said I want more of? (more clients, more time, more income, more impact, etc.) 7. What am I working toward in the next 1-2 years, if I've mentioned it? 8. Is there anything I've said I dream about or aspire to, even if it feels ambitious? Format your answer as a clean numbered list. Only include what you actually know from our conversations — if you don't have information on something, just say "not discussed."
Prompt 3 — What I Don't Like
▾

The most underrated prompt. This is where Claude learns what not to do — your pet peeves, corrections you've made, formats you hate. This is what makes it feel like it really knows you.

Copy → paste into ChatGPT
I'm setting up a new AI assistant and need you to export what you know about my preferences, pet peeves, and the times you've had to adjust your behavior for me. This one is really important — please be honest and specific. 1. What are things I've explicitly told you NOT to do? (e.g., "stop using bullet points", "don't be so formal") 2. What corrections have I made to your responses? (times I said something like "that's not right", "that's not my style", "too long", "wrong tone") 3. What topics or types of content have I said I don't like or don't want? 4. What frustrates me — about AI responses, about work, about people, about anything I've vented about? 5. What writing style or tone do I NOT like? (e.g., too corporate, too casual, too long, too short) 6. Have I ever said something like "I hate when..." or "never do this" — if so, what? 7. Are there any words, phrases, or formats I've asked you to avoid? 8. Is there anything about how I like to communicate that I've had to teach you over time? Format as a numbered list. Be specific — vague answers aren't helpful here. If you don't have information on something, say "not discussed."
What Andre recommends:  Don't rush this one. If ChatGPT's answer feels thin, ask it: "What's one time I pushed back on one of your responses? What did I say?" — that usually unlocks better answers.
Prompt 4 — How I Work
▾

How you communicate, what tools you use, your natural writing voice, how you like responses structured. This is what makes Claude feel like a good working partner.

Copy → paste into ChatGPT
I'm setting up a new AI assistant and need you to export what you know about how I work and how I like to communicate. Based on our conversations, please answer: 1. How do I like AI responses formatted? (bullet points, paragraphs, short, long, with headers, without?) 2. What tone do I prefer when someone is talking to me or writing for me? (professional, casual, direct, warm, funny?) 3. How do I prefer to give instructions — detailed and specific, or loose and open-ended? 4. Do I tend to work quickly and want fast answers, or do I take my time and want thorough explanations? 5. When I'm writing something (emails, posts, messages) — what's my natural writing voice like? 6. How do I handle feedback — do I like directness, or do I prefer a softer approach? 7. What times of day or work patterns have I mentioned? (early bird, night owl, busy on certain days, etc.) 8. Do I work alone mostly, or do I collaborate with a team? What do I prefer? 9. What tools or apps do I use regularly that I've mentioned? (email, project management, social media, etc.) 10. How do I describe my ideal version of productivity or a good work day? Format as a numbered list. Use real examples from our conversations where possible. Say "not discussed" if you genuinely don't have information on something.
Prompt 5 — My Business
▾

Your business in detail — who you serve, what you offer, how you get clients, what your clients are struggling with, and your positioning in the market.

Copy → paste into ChatGPT
I'm setting up a new AI assistant and need you to export what you know about my business and the people I work with. Based on our conversations, please answer: 1. What is my business or what service/product do I offer? Describe it clearly. 2. Who are my ideal clients or customers? (type of person, industry, size of business, demographics) 3. How do I find or attract clients — what's my main way of getting new business? 4. What does a typical client engagement or project look like for me? (how long, what's involved, how it ends) 5. What do my clients usually come to me struggling with, and what do they get from working with me? 6. Do I have any competitors or others in my space that I've mentioned? 7. What prices, packages, or offers do I have, if I've mentioned them? 8. What platforms or channels do I use to promote my work? (Instagram, LinkedIn, email list, website, etc.) 9. Have I mentioned any clients, case studies, or success stories? 10. Is there anything specific about my industry or niche that's important to understand? Format as a numbered list. Include real specifics from our conversations. Say "not discussed" if something wasn't covered.
Prompt 6 — My Projects & Tasks
▾

This one builds your actual work folders inside Claude. After you paste the answer back, Claude will create a dedicated folder for each of your recurring tasks — pre-loaded with everything it knows about how you do that work.

Copy → paste into ChatGPT
I'm setting up a new AI assistant and need you to export what you know about the actual work I do — my recurring projects, tasks, and workflows. This is the most practical one, so please be as specific as possible. 1. What are the main types of work I do regularly? List each one separately (e.g., "resume reviews", "client onboarding calls", "monthly reports") 2. For each type of work you listed — describe what's typically involved. What does the task look like from start to finish? 3. Are there any templates, frameworks, or processes I use or have mentioned for any of these tasks? 4. Which of these tasks do I seem to do most often or find most important? 5. Which tasks have I mentioned finding difficult, time-consuming, or frustrating? 6. Are there any projects I'm currently working on (not just recurring tasks, but specific ongoing initiatives)? 7. Have I mentioned any tools or documents I use for specific tasks? (e.g., "uses Google Docs for contracts", "tracks clients in a spreadsheet") 8. Are there tasks I've asked you to help with repeatedly — that we have a pattern of working on together? For your answer, please structure it like this: RECURRING TASKS: - [Task name]: [What it involves, how I approach it, any tools or templates I use] CURRENT PROJECTS: - [Project name]: [What it is, where it stands, what the goal is] TOOLS & DOCUMENTS: - [Tool or doc]: [What I use it for] Be specific. This is going to become my working file system in my new AI assistant.
📁 After you paste this back into Cowork, Claude will build your project folders automatically. You'll see a file structure like the one below appear in your project.

Fill the Gaps
A few quick questions Claude will ask you directly
What happens here

After all 6 prompts, Claude will ask you a handful of questions that ChatGPT wouldn't know — like your website, what you most want help with daily, and how you like responses. Answer them conversationally, one at a time, right inside Cowork. No copy-pasting needed.

What Claude will ask
  • What's your website URL?
  • What do you most want Claude to help you with day-to-day?
  • Anything about AI that's annoyed you or hasn't worked?
  • Do you prefer short punchy answers or detailed thorough ones?
  • Anything else Claude should know that we haven't covered?
Phase 2 complete — I answered Claude's questions
▾

Just answer in plain language inside Cowork. No right or wrong answers. Claude will update your profile files as you go.

3
The Wow Test
3 real tasks — watch how different it feels
Why this matters

This is the moment everything clicks. You'll ask Claude to do 3 tasks you actually do in your work. The goal isn't just to see if it works — it's to feel the difference between a Claude that knows you and one that doesn't. That feeling is what you'll get every day from now on.

1
The Email Test
Ask Claude to draft a follow-up email to a recent client. Notice how it uses your tone, your voice, your business context — without you explaining any of it.
2
The Content Test
Ask Claude to write a short social media post about your business or something you help clients with. Watch it capture your personality without being prompted.
3
The Work Test
Ask Claude to help with one of your most common recurring tasks. It already has your approach, standards, and preferences loaded from Prompt 6.
Setup complete — Claude knows me 🎉
▾

You're done. From now on, every Claude Cowork session in this project starts with full context about who you are, how you work, and what you do. No more re-explaining yourself.

What Andre recommends:  Revisit your my-brain/ files every few months as your business evolves. The more current it stays, the more useful Claude becomes.
