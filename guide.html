Before you start
Get set up in 5 minutes — no tech knowledge needed
What you'll need
  • A Claude account — free or paid (sign up at claude.ai)
  • Either claude.ai (browser or desktop app) or Claude Code — pick one below
  • About 45–60 minutes of uninterrupted time
Two ways to run this setup — pick one
  • claude.ai: Go to claude.ai, create a new Project, paste the contents of CLAUDE.md as Project Instructions, and upload the files in the prompts/ folder as knowledge. Then start a conversation.
  • Claude Code: Open this folder in Claude Code. CLAUDE.md is read automatically and Claude will greet you on your first message.

Not sure which? Use claude.ai if you want to chat. Use Claude Code if you want the full file-based system with agents, skills, and templates written directly to your folder.

What Andre recommends:  Don't skip Phase 0. It takes 5 minutes and prevents 90% of confusion later. The rest is just copy, paste, repeat.

Your Context
6 prompts · answer directly or use another AI tool · Claude builds your profile as you go
How this phase works

Claude gives you one prompt at a time. You can answer directly — just talk through it in your own words. Or, if you already use another AI tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) and have a history there, paste the prompt in and bring back what it says about you. Either way works. Claude builds your profile from whatever you share.

💡 You can use the prompts below as a reference. If an answer feels thin or vague, ask for more detail — or just add it yourself in your own words.
Prompt 1 — Who I Am
▾

This prompt covers who you are professionally — your name, role, background, and personality. It's the foundation everything else builds on. Answer directly or paste it into another AI and bring back the answer.

Copy → use directly or paste into another AI
Answer the following about me based on everything you know from our conversations. Be specific — real details only. 1. Who am I professionally — name, role, type of work, and whether I'm employed, freelance, or running my own business? 2. How would you describe my background and what I actually do, in 3–5 sentences? 3. What have I said makes me different or strongest at what I do? And how would you describe my personality in 2–3 sentences?
↩ Once you have an answer, read it carefully. If something feels wrong or incomplete, correct it before sharing it with Claude.
Prompt 2 — My Goals & Desires
▾

This one captures what you're working toward — professional goals, personal aspirations, what success means to you, and what you want more of.

Copy → use directly or paste into another AI
Answer the following about me based on everything you know from our conversations. Be specific — real details only. 1. What are my main professional goals right now? 2. What problems am I actively trying to solve, and what does success look like in the next 1–2 years? 3. What have I said I want more of — clients, income, time, visibility, something else?
Prompt 3 — What I Don't Like
▾

The most underrated prompt. This is where Claude learns what not to do — your pet peeves, corrections you've made, formats you hate. This is what makes it feel like it really knows you.

Copy → use directly or paste into another AI
Answer the following about me based on everything you know from our conversations. Be specific — real details only. 1. What have I explicitly asked you not to do — any "stop doing X" or "never do Y" corrections? 2. What feedback have I given on your responses — too long, wrong tone, not my style, over-explained? 3. Are there specific words, phrases, or formats I've asked you to avoid?
What Andre recommends:  Don't rush this one. If the answer feels thin, dig deeper — ask: "What's one time I pushed back on one of your responses? What did I say?" That usually unlocks better answers.
Prompt 4 — How I Work
▾

How you communicate, what tools you use, your natural writing voice, how you like responses structured. This is what makes Claude feel like a good working partner.

Copy → use directly or paste into another AI
Answer the following about me based on everything you know from our conversations. Be specific — real details only. 1. How do I like responses formatted — bullet points, paragraphs, headers, short, long? 2. What tone works best — casual, direct, formal, warm, blunt? 3. What's my natural writing voice like when I write emails, posts, or messages? 4. What tools and apps do I use regularly?
Prompt 5 — My Work Context
▾

Your work context — what you do, who you work with, what a typical engagement looks like, and how you find new work. This applies whether you're employed, freelance, or running your own thing.

Copy → use directly or paste into another AI
Answer the following about me based on everything you know from our conversations. Be specific — real details only. My Work Context 1. What do I do and what do I offer — describe my work clearly? 2. Who do I work for or with, and what do they typically need from me? 3. What does a typical project or engagement look like — start to finish? 4. How do I find or attract new work? What platforms or channels am I active on?
Prompt 6 — My Projects & Tasks
▾

This one builds your actual work folders inside Claude. After you paste the answer back, Claude will create a dedicated folder for each of your recurring tasks — pre-loaded with everything it knows about how you do that work.

Copy → use directly or paste into another AI
Answer the following about me based on everything you know from our conversations. Be specific — real details only. 1. What are the main types of work I do regularly? List each one separately. 2. For each — what does it involve from start to finish? 3. Are there templates, frameworks, or processes I use for any of these? 4. What projects am I currently working on — specific ongoing initiatives, not just recurring tasks? Structure your answer like this: RECURRING TASKS: - [Task name]: [What it involves, how I approach it, any tools or templates I use] CURRENT PROJECTS: - [Project name]: [What it is, where it stands, what the goal is] TOOLS & DOCUMENTS: - [Tool or doc]: [What I use it for]
📁 After you share this answer with Claude, it will build your project folders automatically. In Claude Code, you'll see the file structure appear in your folder. In claude.ai, Claude will describe the structure it's creating.

2
Fill the Gaps
A few quick questions Claude will ask you directly
What happens here

After all 6 prompts, Claude will ask you a handful of questions that only you can answer — like your website, what you most want help with daily, and how you like responses. Answer them conversationally, one at a time. No copy-pasting needed.

What Claude will ask
  • What's your website URL?
  • What do you most want Claude to help you with day-to-day?
  • Anything about AI that's annoyed you or hasn't worked?
  • Do you prefer short punchy answers or detailed thorough ones?
  • Anything else Claude should know that we haven't covered?
Phase 2 complete — I answered Claude's questions
▾

Just answer in plain language. No right or wrong answers. Claude will update your profile files as you go.

3
The Wow Test
3 real tasks — watch how different it feels
Why this matters

This is the moment everything clicks. You'll ask Claude to do 3 tasks you actually do in your work. The goal isn't just to see if it works — it's to feel the difference between a Claude that knows you and one that doesn't. That feeling is what you'll get every day from now on.

1
The Personalized Message
Claude drafts a message for you unprompted — a follow-up, client note, or quick pitch in your actual voice. It won't ask what you do, who your clients are, or how you like things written. It already knows.
2
The Agent
Claude invokes one of the custom agents it built for you — a persona or perspective tailored to your dominant use case. Watch it respond as someone deeply familiar with your type of work.
3
The Skill
Claude triggers one of the skills it built for a task you do regularly. You say a single phrase — it executes a full, structured response built around your specific workflow.
Setup complete — Claude knows me 🎉
▾

You're done. From now on, every Claude session in this project starts with full context about who you are, how you work, and what you do — plus agents, skills, and templates built around your actual work. No more re-explaining yourself.

What Andre recommends:  Revisit your my-brain/ files every few months as your business evolves. The more current it stays, the more useful Claude becomes.
