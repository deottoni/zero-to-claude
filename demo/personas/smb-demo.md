## Paste for Round 1 (You & your work)

I'm Sam Okafor, founder and owner of Bright Leaf Coffee Co. — a specialty coffee roaster with 2 cafe locations and a growing online subscription box. I've been in coffee for 8 years, started roasting out of a garage before opening the first cafe. We do about $1.8M in revenue now across the cafes, wholesale accounts, and the subscription box. What makes us different is I'm obsessive about sourcing and quality — direct relationships with growers, no corporate coffee-speak, no compromising on the actual product to cut costs. My main goal right now is growing the subscription box 3x in the next 18 months without letting the in-cafe experience slip — that's the part I'm most protective of.

## Paste for Round 4 (Team & functions — SMB only)

We're 7 people: me (owner, also still roasting), Priya who's admin and ops coordinator, Jamal who does marketing and social, two cafe shift leads, one wholesale/sales rep, and a part-time bookkeeper. Most big decisions run through me, but marketing and day-to-day cafe ops are pretty independent — Jamal and the shift leads don't need to check with me for normal stuff.

The functions that need the most help right now: Marketing, Sales, Ops, and honestly Finance too.
- Marketing: Jamal spends hours every week writing subscription emails and social captions from scratch, and honestly the brand voice comes out inconsistent depending on how rushed he is.
- Sales (wholesale): our wholesale rep is slow and inconsistent following up with accounts — we've lost leads just from not following up in time.
- Ops: there's no real SOP for onboarding new cafe staff. It's all in my head, so training a new hire takes way longer than it should and depends on whoever happens to train them.
