How it works

1

Get the folder. Clone this repo or download it as a ZIP.

2

Open it in Claude. Claude Code, Cowork, or a new claude.ai Project — CLAUDE.md works the same way in each.

3

Say "let's go." Claude asks two quick questions — which track, and whether it can write files here — then runs the whole setup with you: a few short rounds of questions, then a personal or company "brain," plus agents, skills, and templates built around your real work.

This page is just a preview. Claude tracks your progress in the conversation itself — there's nothing to check off here.
